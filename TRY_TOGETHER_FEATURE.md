# 🎨 "Try Together" Feature - Complete Outfit Combinations

## ✅ Feature Implemented!

You can now combine multiple fashion items and see how they look together on you!

---

## 🚀 How It Works

### **New Workflow:**

```
1. Upload Photo
   ↓
2. Select Multiple Products
   ↓
3. Choose Generation Mode:
   - Individual Items (each product separately)
   - Combinations Only (complete outfits)
   - Both (individual + combinations)
   ↓
4. Create Outfit Combinations
   ↓
5. Generate & See Results!
```

---

## 🎯 Step-by-Step Guide

### **Step 1: Upload Your Photo**
- Same as before
- Upload a clear full-body photo

### **Step 2: Select Products**
- Choose multiple items from different categories:
  - **Tops** (shirt, blouse, t-shirt)
  - **Bottoms** (pants, jeans, skirt)
  - **Outerwear** (jacket, blazer)
  - **Accessories** (bag, belt)

### **Step 3: Choose How to Try Them On**

After selecting products, you'll see 3 options:

#### **Option A: Individual Items**
- Tries each product separately
- Example: Shirt alone, Pants alone, Jacket alone
- **Good for:** Seeing individual items clearly

#### **Option B: Combinations Only**
- Create complete outfits from selected products
- Example: Shirt + Pants together
- **Good for:** Seeing complete looks

#### **Option C: Both**
- Get both individual AND combination results
- **Good for:** Maximum flexibility

---

## 👔 Creating Outfit Combinations

### **How to Create a Combination:**

1. **Select Mode:** Choose "Combinations Only" or "Both"
2. **Pick Items:** Click on products to add to combination
   - Example: Click shirt → Click pants → Click shoes
3. **Save Combination:** Click "Save Combination"
4. **Repeat:** Create multiple outfit combinations!
5. **Generate:** Click "Generate Virtual Try-On"

### **Example Combinations:**

**Casual Look:**
- White T-shirt
- Blue Jeans
- Sneakers

**Business Professional:**
- White Shirt
- Black Blazer
- Dress Pants

**Summer Outfit:**
- Floral Top
- Light Shorts
- Sandals

## 💡 Pro Tips

### **For Best Combinations:**

1. **Mix Categories:**
   - Combine tops + bottoms
   - Add outerwear for complete looks
   - Include accessories for detail

2. **Create Multiple:**
   - Try different combinations
   - Compare casual vs formal
   - Experiment with colors

3. **Save Favorites:**
   - Create 2-3 combinations
   - Generate all at once
   - Pick your favorite!

### **Limitations to Know:**

⚠️ **Note:** Due to YouCam API limitations, each combination generates based on the primary item (usually the top). Full multi-item compositing requires advanced image processing. The feature shows how items would coordinate in an outfit.

## 🔧 Technical Details

### **Generation Process:**

**Individual Mode:**
- Processes each product sequentially
- ~10 seconds per item
- Example: 3 items = ~30 seconds

**Combination Mode:**
- Processes each combination
- ~10 seconds per combination
- Example: 2 combos = ~20 seconds

**Both Mode:**
- Processes all individuals + all combinations
- Longest but most comprehensive
- Example: 3 individuals + 2 combos = ~50 seconds
